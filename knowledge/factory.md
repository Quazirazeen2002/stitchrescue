# DEMO Factory Quality SOP

Document ID: SOP-DEMO-001
Status: SIMULATED — NOT AN APPROVED FACTORY SOP

## Fabric defect handling

When an inspector suspects a fabric defect,
record the fabric roll ID, order ID, defect
description and inspection evidence.

## Oil stain investigation

Place the potentially affected fabric on hold.

Request quality-team verification.

Review the associated batch and machine records.

Do not assume that an oil stain was caused by
a particular machine without supporting evidence.

## Rework decision

Rework requires quality-team authorization.

Any proposed cleaning procedure must be checked
against the actual material specifications and
the buyer's approved requirements.

## Shipment risk

Before approving rework or replacement,
calculate remaining production requirements,
available capacity and shipment constraints.

## Escalation

If buyer acceptance criteria are unavailable,
escalate the decision to the quality manager.

Do not automatically release affected garments.
